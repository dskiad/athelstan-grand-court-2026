Annual Meetings

Two annual gatherings will take place at Freemasons’ Hall, 80 London Road, Leicester, LE2 0RA.

Friday • 23 October 2026

Annual Meeting of the Kings Council of the Ancient Masonic Order of Alfred the Great

Time: 18:00 hours
Venue: Freemasons’ Hall, 80 London Road, Leicester, LE2 0RA
Saturday • 24 October 2026

Annual Assembly of the Grand Court of the Masonic Order of Athelstan

Time: 11:00 hours
Venue: Freemasons’ Hall, 80 London Road, Leicester, LE2 0RA

Brethren of both Orders are cordially and fraternally invited to attend these annual gatherings and to join the Most Worshipful Grand Master, Charles J. Aspinell, GCAG, and the Most Worshipful Sovereign Grand Master, Christopher W. G. Ansell, GCAG, as honour and recognition are rendered to Brethren to be preferred to Grand Rank.

Registration & Grand Banquets

Members wishing to attend Kings Council (AMOAG) and/or Grand Court may use the official booking platform. The same platform is used to book and pay for dining, or to register attendance or apology without partaking of the Festive Board.

Members should log in using the email address registered with the MOA, select “Grand Court 2026”, and follow the on-screen instructions.
